## Longer announcement

Rachel 1.2 is out, and I'm rather pleased with this one.

Meet Saturday: painted felt, drawn cards and a Saturday-morning cartoon look,
with light and dark appearances. It's one of five tables you can choose from.

I've also brought Rachel to Apple silicon Macs. You can bring your iPhone,
iPad or Mac to the same game, and invite Game Center friends right from the
host lobby. If a guest disconnects, their seat and hand are kept while the
host's game stays open, so they can rejoin when invited back.

The game is still about getting rid of your cards while everyone else tries
to give you more. Play solo offline against fourteen characters, or bring
friends together nearby without an account or internet connection.

One purchase, the whole game. No ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Pull up a chair: https://rachel.stevehill.xyz
